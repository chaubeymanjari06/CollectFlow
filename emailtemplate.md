# 4. Best Practices for High Email Deliverability in India

To ensure your cold email campaigns land in the primary inbox (and not the spam or promotions tab):

1. **Custom Domain & Warm-Up**:
   - Send cold emails from a secondary domain (e.g., `getcollectflow.in` or `collectflow.co`), never your root app domain.
   - Configure **SPF, DKIM, and DMARC** DNS records with a strict alignment policy.
   - Warm up each inbox for 14 days using automated warm-up software (keep sending volume below 35 emails/inbox/day).
2. **Plain Text Over Heavy HTML**:
   - Indian B2B executives ignore glossy marketing newsletters with heavy graphics.
   - Keep emails strictly text-based with minimal formatting (looks like a personal 1-to-1 email from a founder).
3. **Optimal Sending Times (Indian Standard Time)**:
   - **Best Days**: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.
   - **Slot 1 (Morning Pulse)**: 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM IST (when owners review morning dispatch and bank balances).
   - **Slot 2 (Post-Lunch Review)**: 3:30 PM to 5:00 PM IST (before evening factory dispatches).
   - Avoid sending on Monday mornings (meetings) and Saturday/Sunday.
4. **Compliance & Legal Hygiene**:
   - Include a physical business address in the footer.
   - Include a 1-click unsubscribe or clear opt-out statement (*"Reply UNSUBSCRIBE to stop receiving updates"*), fully compliant with the **DPDP Act 2023** and **CAN-SPAM** regulations.
